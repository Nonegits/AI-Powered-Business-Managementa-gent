# OrbitCart Customer Support Policy

## Return requests
Support agents verify the order date, category-specific return window, serial number when applicable, and customer-provided issue before approving a return. Return reasons must use the standard reason codes: defect, wrong item, damaged in transit, inaccurate listing, size or fit, and changed mind.

## Escalations
When a product has three or more similar complaints in a seven-day period, support sends a product-quality alert to merchandising and fulfillment.
