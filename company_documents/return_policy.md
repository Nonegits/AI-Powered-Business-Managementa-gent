# OrbitCart Return Policy

## Standard returns
Customers may return eligible items within 15 calendar days of delivery. The item must include its invoice, original accessories, and packaging. Refunds are returned to the original payment method after the return passes inspection.

## Electronics
Electronics, including laptops, phones, and audio products, are eligible for return within 7 calendar days of delivery. Devices must be returned with serial-number labels intact and all supplied accessories. Products with physical damage caused after delivery are not eligible. A verified manufacturing defect qualifies for replacement or refund.

## Non-returnable items
Opened personal-care items, products damaged by customer misuse, and items without a verifiable order record cannot be returned.
