# OrbitCart Shipping Policy

## Delivery expectations
Orders are dispatched within one business day. Metro locations generally receive shipments in two to four business days. Delivery delays caused by address corrections or customer unavailability are communicated by email and SMS.

## Damaged deliveries
Customers should report visibly damaged packages within 48 hours and attach photographs of the package and product. The support team records a damage code before arranging a replacement or return.
