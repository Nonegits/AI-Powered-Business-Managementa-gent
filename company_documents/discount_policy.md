# OrbitCart Discount Policy

### Disclaimer: These Policies are made by AI for legitimate values that can be used for policy making.

## Discount limits
Standard promotions range from 5% to 15%. Discounts above 15% require approval from the merchandising lead. Discounted products remain eligible for return when they meet the applicable category policy.

## Product listings
Merchandising must review customer feedback, defect reports, and return reasons before extending a promotion on any product with a return rate above its category baseline.
